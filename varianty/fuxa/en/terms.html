2. How a project starts

  1. You tell me what you need. I send you a quote with the price, scope and timeline.
  2. For a website, I first design your homepage for free. If you don't like the design or change your mind, you don't pay anything and you don't owe me anything.
  3. The contract is made when you let me know after the design that you want to continue. For graphic design, when you confirm the quote.
  4. We can agree by email, text message, phone or in person. If we agree by phone or in person, I'll send you a summary by email or text message so we both have it in writing.

3. Price and payment

  1. The price is the one in the quote. I'm not a VAT payer, so there's no VAT on my invoice.
  2. A website is paid in two parts. You pay half after approving the design and the other half after approving the finished website. I launch the website once the second payment arrives.
  3. Graphic design is paid the same way. You pay half after confirming the quote and the other half after approving the finished work. I send you the final files once you've paid.
  4. The due date is on the invoice.
  5. For business clients, the first half is non-refundable because it covers work I've already done. For consumers, section 9 applies.
  6. You pay for the domain and hosting separately, and the quote says how much. If I built your website and look after it, they're included in the price of website care.

4. Working together

  1. Send me information about your business, your logo, photos and any access I need for the work. I write the copy and you check it.
  2. You're responsible for the materials you send me. You must have the rights to them, especially to photos and logos.
  3. You can send feedback as many times as you like, as long as it's within the scope of the quote. Anything extra (more pages, new features) I'll price first and do once you agree.
  4. If I'm waiting for your materials or approval, the timeline moves by that time.
  5. If I don't hear from you for more than 60 days, I can end the project. I'll warn you by email beforehand. Money already paid for the work I've done up to that point isn't refunded.

5. Handover and edits after launch

  1. The website is handed over when you approve it and I launch it on your domain.
  2. After launch, small edits are included for 30 days on a smaller website and 45 days on a larger one. A small edit is, for example, changing text, swapping a photo or moving an element. New pages and features aren't small edits.
  3. If a bug I caused appears on the website, I'll fix it for free even after this period.
  4. I'm not responsible for errors caused by someone else's changes or for outages of services the website runs on (hosting, booking systems, payment gateways and similar).

6. Copyright

  1. The websites and graphics I create are my copyrighted work. Once you've paid the full price, you can use them without a time limit, edit them and have someone else edit them.
  2. I can show the finished work in my portfolio and on social media. I can reuse general parts of the code on other websites.
  3. The website footer has a small "Created by FuxaStudio" link. If you don't want it there, let me know and I'll remove it.
  4. Photos, fonts, templates and plugins from other authors have their own licenses, and their terms apply.

7. Website care

  1. You pay for website care in advance for a period you choose: a month, six months or a year. The price is in the quote.
  2. What website care includes is in the quote. For websites I built, the domain and hosting are included.
  3. You can cancel website care at any time. It ends at the end of the paid period and the rest of the period isn't refunded.
  4. If you don't pay for the next period, I'll end website care. For websites where I pay for the domain and hosting, I'll warn you by email first so the website doesn't stop working.
  5. I can transfer the domain, hosting and website to you or someone else at any time.

8. Liability

For business clients, my liability for damages is limited to the price of the project. None of this limits the rights consumers have under the law.

9. For consumers

A consumer is a person who orders work from me for themselves, not for their business.

  1. Withdrawal within 14 days. You can withdraw from the contract within 14 days of it being made, without giving a reason. Just let me know by email or text message. You can also use the form below.
  2. If you ask me to start working within those 14 days and then withdraw, you pay me a proportional part for the work already done.
  3. If, with your consent, I finish all the work within 14 days, the right to withdraw ends.
  4. I'll refund your money within 14 days of your withdrawal, the same way you paid.
  5. Complaints. Let me know about a defect by email or text message, or call me. I'll handle it as soon as possible, within 30 days at the latest.
  6. Disputes. If we can't agree, you can turn to the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (adr.coi.cz), which resolves consumer disputes out of court.
