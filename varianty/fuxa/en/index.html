How it
works.

You don't pay anything until you approve the design. Then you pay half, once you've seen it and want to go ahead.

01

Getting in touch

You message me

Just a few sentences about what you do and what the website should do. We'll go over the details and I'll send you a quote with the price and timeline.

04

Approval

Finished website

You go through the website on your phone and computer. Anything you don't like, I'll change. Once everything is the way you want it, you approve the website.

02

Design

Free design

I design your homepage and send it to you as a link. It doesn't have to be exactly what you had in mind right away. The point is whether it makes sense to you and you want to continue. If not, you don't pay anything.

05

Launch

Your website goes live

I set up basic SEO so Google can find you and go through the whole website one more time. Then you pay the second half and I launch the website on your domain.

03

Building

I build your website

You pay half the price and I build the whole website. You send your feedback right on the live version, which you can click through.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

What you should know before we start. Didn't find your answer? Get in touch.

01 How long does it take to make a website?

A smaller website is usually ready in 1⁠–⁠2 weeks, a larger one in 2⁠–⁠3 weeks after I get everything from you. You'll find the exact timeline in the quote. If the design or the finished website is waiting for your approval, the timeline moves by that time.

02 Can I add something to a smaller website?

Yes. Visitor analytics, Google profile setup, a second language, a blog or anything else, I can add to any website. Just let me know and I'll add the price to the quote.

03 Do your prices include VAT?

I'm not a VAT payer, so there's no VAT on my invoice.

04 I don't have any text or photos. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I'll write the text myself from what you tell me about your business. If you're nearby, I can stop by and take a few photos on my phone. If you're farther away, I'll tell you what to photograph. For professional photos, a photographer is the better choice.

05 Do you also make logos and other graphics?

Yes, even if I'm not building your website. Logos, business cards, social media posts, banners, menus or T-shirt prints. Smaller things start at €40 and you'll get the exact price in the quote. If I make your website too, everything fits together.

06 Do I pay anything every year?

You pay for the domain and hosting separately. The quote says how much it'll be. If I built your website and look after it (from €12 a month), they're included.

07 Who owns the domain and the website?

You do. I can register the domain and hosting in your name from the start. If you don't want to deal with it, leave them with me as part of website care. I can transfer everything to you or someone else at any time.

08 Can I edit the website myself?

With WordPress and Framer, yes, you can change text, photos and posts yourself. With a custom-coded website, you send me the changes. Small edits are included after launch, 30 days for a smaller website and 45 for a larger one. After that, we'll agree on it or it's part of website care.

09 Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. We handle everything by email or video call, in English, Czech or Slovak. That's how I made websites for a café in Bratislava and an international consulting firm. If you're in Pardubice or Chrudim, I'm happy to meet in person.
