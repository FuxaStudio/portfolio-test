I run the business on my own, so I'm the only one who works with your data. If you have any questions, just write to me.

What data and why

Inquiries from the form or by email
Your name, email or phone and what you write to me. I need them to reply and send you a quote. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.

Projects
Contact and billing details (name, company, address, company ID) and our communication about the project. I need them to do the project and issue invoices. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

Invoices
I'm required by law to keep invoices. Legal basis: legal obligation.

Website traffic
If you allow analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how you use the website (which pages you viewed, what device you used, where you came from). I can't tell your name or contact details from it. Legal basis: your consent. Without consent, Google Analytics doesn't run. More on the Cookies page.

How long I keep data

  • Inquiries that didn't turn into a project: 2 years, in case you get in touch again.
  • Communication about a project: while we work together and 3 years after, in case anything needs sorting out.
  • Invoices: 10 years, as required by law.
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.

Who else has access to your data

I don't sell your data or give it to anyone. But I use services your data passes through:

  • Web3Forms (run by the Indian company Web3Creative), which sends form inquiries to my email. It stores inquiries for up to 3 years.
  • Seznam.cz, where I have my business email.
  • Fakturoid, which I use for invoicing.
  • Cloudflare, where the website runs.
  • Google, if you allow analytics cookies.
  • My accountant, who handles my taxes.

Web3Forms, Cloudflare and Google also process data outside the EU. Cloudflare and Google under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Web3Forms under standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

Your rights

You can ask me to tell you what data I have about you, correct it or delete it. You can ask me to stop processing it or to send it to you in a file. You can change your cookie consent at any time via "Cookie settings" in the website footer.

Just write to fuxa@fuxastudio.cz and I'll reply within a month. Data I'm required by law to keep (invoices) I can't delete.

If you're not happy with something, write to me and we'll sort it out. You can also file a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz) or with the data protection authority in your country.
