Common questions

What people ask most

How much does a website cost?

A one-page site works out at €400 to €600, a multi-page site at €800 to €1,150. More demanding projects, a shop or a booking system for example, I quote individually. These are guide ranges and the binding price is the one in your quote.

What moves the final price?

Mostly how much work is left to me. With your own copy, photos and a clear idea you land at the bottom of the range. If I have to think up and deliver all of it, at the top. The number of pages matters too, and extra functions like bookings or a second language.

How long does it take?

Within a month of getting your materials, usually sooner. A one-page site is normally done in one to two weeks, a multi-page site in two to three. The deadline goes into the written quote and it holds the same way the price does.

Do you build in WordPress, in Framer or hand-coded?

All three, depending on the project. Hand-coded makes sense when the site mainly has to be fast and cost nothing to run. WordPress when you want to edit the content yourself. Framer when the site has to be out quickly and mainly has to look good. Which one makes more sense for you goes straight into the quote.

Do you design from scratch or use templates?

I design from scratch. If you’ve already picked a template and want to build on it, that’s fine too. I’ll adapt it to your brand. That’s how the Global Accord Strategies site was made.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

On WordPress and Framer yes, you change copy, photos and articles in the admin. On a hand-coded site you send the changes to me. For the first 30 days after launch small changes are included, after that by arrangement or under care and development.

Will you redesign a site I already have?

Yes, a redesign is ordinary work. What works on the site stays, what holds it back gets rebuilt. I can handle a site you’ve lost access to. That is how I took over the whole site for Mlýn Janderov, domain and hosting included.

Does working remotely actually work?

It does, including abroad. Mattina Café in Bratislava and the site for an international consultancy were both built for the same client, entirely at a distance, over Upwork, email and calls. We’ve never met in person and she still came back with another job.

Do you work in English?

Yes. English is my working language and I write the copy for client sites in it myself. I’m in Central European time, so we overlap with the whole of Europe on any working day.

How does payment work?

By invoice, in two parts. I design your homepage first and you pay nothing until then. If you like the design, you pay half and I build the rest of the site. The other half is due once you've approved the finished site, before it goes live. You never pay everything up front.
