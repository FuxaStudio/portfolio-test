Who I am

The very first site I built was for Svídnický extrém. A friend needed people to sign up for the race online instead of through Facebook. The site was live in 18 days. I never went into a company on purpose. The result is on me. There’s nobody for me to point at, and that’s exactly how I want it.

Limits

What I do not do

The fastest way to find out whether we’ll get on is to know where I don’t go.

  • Photography. Photos decide how a site turns out and I can’t take them. I’ll tell you what we need and point you to someone. If you have your own and they’re usable, we work with those.
  • Social media and paid ads. I build the site so the ads have somewhere to send people. The ads themselves are not my work.
  • Monthly SEO packages. Whatever can be done for search engines while building the site, I do. I will not promise you first place on Google for a monthly fee. Nobody can guarantee that.
  • Sites “by the end of the week”. When the deadline is unrealistic, so is the result. I’d rather say so up front.
Risk

“And what if we part ways?”

Nobody asks me this out loud, and it decides whether you write to me at all. So here’s how it’s covered.

Who will own the domain and hosting?

However you want. Some clients leave the running of it to me so they have nothing to deal with. Anyone who wants it all in their own name gets it set up that way from the start. And at any point later I transfer the lot to you or to somebody else. No arguing about it.

Who would be able to carry on with the site?

I build in ordinary technologies, hand-coded, on WordPress or in Framer. No system of my own that only I understand. Any developer can pick it up.

What if the site disappears along with the hosting?

I keep a complete current copy of every site on my own machine as well. If anything happened to the hosting, I put the site back up somewhere else. The work doesn’t disappear.

And if I haven’t got back to you within 24 hours, something has happened. Call me. It’s not normal.
