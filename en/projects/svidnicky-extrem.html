From nothing to a live site in 18 days

A new website for an obstacle course race and its year-round training sessions. It brought 126 clicks from Google in three months.

Client
Svídnický extrém
Field
Sporting events and training
Scope
Hand-coded site, copy and race registration
Live site
svidnickyextrem.cz ↗

The client

A group of friends from Svídnice, the village I live in. They run an obstacle course race called Svídnický extrém, a summer event for local families when the school holidays start, and training sessions all year round. Obstacle course racing is the sport Tough Mudder and Spartan Race made familiar, here at village scale. Until then, email and Facebook had been enough for them.

Where it started

No website at all. Everything lived in emails and on Facebook. The goal was to get all of it into one place and to wire online race registration into the site.

How it was built

There was almost nothing to work from. I wrote the copy myself and the client corrected it against reality. My “streams” turned into rivers, and instead of the obstacles I had imagined, real ones arrived, a monkey-bar traverse among them. “Writing this stuff down is hard,” he told me at the start, so instead of long emails we went through the brief in person, and I drove out to take the photos myself. (client quote translated from Czech)

Race registration runs through sport:base, the platform Czech races use for entries and start lists. As soon as the client had his race approved there, he sent me the link. It was wired into the site the next morning.

The training videos took the longest. Sent over WhatsApp they lost quality, so we moved them to YouTube in the end. The videos play at full quality and the client manages them himself, without touching the site.
