A website for a café that didn’t exist yet

A bilingual site for a new café in Bratislava. Live a month before opening, so the marketing had somewhere to send people.

The client came back with a second job.

Mattina Café
Field

The client

A new café in Bratislava. At the time of the brief it was a bare space with a firm opening plan and a mystery campaign running at the address. The owner found me on Upwork. We have never met in person.

Where it started

There were no photos and the menu was still being written. The site had to be finished a month before opening anyway, so the marketing had somewhere to send people.

How it was built

No photos, no prices. The stock hadn’t even been ordered. So we started with stock photography and a fixed structure, and the content was swapped in as material arrived from the café team. For the food I turned down photos off the internet and left an empty space where the croissants were, rather than inventing what was in them.

The mystery marketing was its own chapter. A campaign reading “something is coming” was running at the café address, so the holding page wasn’t allowed to give the address away. And because the opening date wasn’t fixed, I built it without the usual countdown. The client wrote back “great, thank you, yes like this, without the timer.” (translated from Slovak)

The site is fully in Slovak and English. I wrote the copy for the first blog posts, and the client singled them out in her review. When a bug turned up in the language switcher after launch, it was reported in the morning and fixed the same afternoon.

Key decisions

Fixed structure, content on the move

Stock photos only where they don’t lie.

Mystery marketing

The holding page gave away neither the address nor the date.

Two languages

Slovak and English, first blog posts included.

How it ran

< 1 month from contract to a finished site

  1. Day 0
    Contract signed through Upwork.
  2. Ongoing
    Copy, translations and the first blog posts were written as the material came in.
  3. Until opening
    A holding page reading “something is coming”, no address given.
  4. < 1 month
    The finished bilingual site, a month before the planned opening.
