The client

An international consultancy, a startup with no time for a website. This was the second job from the client I’d built Mattina Café for. The whole thing ran remotely again.

Where it started

The consultancy needed a full website and needed it fast. The brief was that they shouldn’t have to build it themselves.

How it was built

The client came back after the Mattina Café site with a second job and two candidate themes. I recommended she not take her favourite. A ring around the cursor, heavy animation and layouts that keep switching now read as “made by AI” to people, even when a human really did build the site. I left the decision to her. The next day she wrote back “OK, we will take the other one.” (translated from Slovak)

Each of the four service areas got its own copy and its own images, so they wouldn’t be four identical pages. On top of that came selecting and retouching the photography, and recolouring the logo to match the site. The client only sent comments.

One thing we agreed on right at the start. No posturing. The company is a startup, so no invented testimonials and no client logos went on the site, only modest and truthful numbers.

Key decisions

The whole site handled

Nine pages, copy and legal pages included.

A theme without the AI look

I recommended against the favourite. The client agreed.

No posturing

A startup with no history doesn’t pretend otherwise.

How it ran

8 days from brief to an approved site

  1. Day 0
    Choosing the theme. Both candidates picked apart in half an hour.
  2. Day 2
    The site filled with working copy. A questionnaire collecting the real data.
  3. Day 6
    Comments worked in, legal pages included. “All done.”
  4. Day 8
    Last round of fixes closed in under 4 hours, site approved.
