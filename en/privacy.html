Privacy policy

Short and without the legalese. What I collect from you, why I need it and what I do with it.

Last updated 24 August 2026

01Who controls the data

I’m Pavel Fuxa, a freelance web designer and developer, business ID 29618843, registered in the Czech Republic. I run fuxastudio.cz myself and I decide myself what happens to your data. I don’t have a data protection officer, so you write to me directly.

Email fuxa@fuxastudio.cz, phone +420 736 131 410.

02The contact form

When you send me an enquiry through the form, I get your name, the text of your message and a contact, meaning an email address, a phone number, or both, depending on what you fill in. The name, the message and at least one contact are required, because without them I can’t answer you or estimate the scope of work.

I process them in order to deal with your enquiry, so for negotiating possible work together. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR, performance of a contract and steps taken before entering into one. I don’t need your consent for that. I won’t send you a newsletter on the basis of the form. That would need separate consent, and the form doesn’t collect it.

If nothing comes of the enquiry, I delete it within twelve months of the last reply. If we do work together, I keep the correspondence for as long as it lasts and for three years after that in case of complaints. Invoices I have to keep longer by law, but that applies to billing details, not to the content of your message.

03Who the data reaches

I don’t sell anyone any data and I don’t pass it to anyone for their own purposes. I do use three services that the data technically passes through.

Service What for
Web3Forms Delivers the submitted form to my inbox. The content of the message including your contact passes through it.
Cloudflare Hosts the site and delivers it to you. Processes technical logs including your IP address.
Google Analytics 4 Traffic statistics without identifying individuals. Only loads if you allow measurement.

Web3Forms, Google and Cloudflare may process data outside the European Union as well. All three operate under the European Commission standard contractual clauses, which permit such a transfer.

04Cookies and measurement

Without your consent the site stores no cookies for measurement or advertising. Google Analytics doesn’t start and the Google script isn’t even downloaded until you click Agree in the banner. If you click Necessary only, nothing extra is downloaded.

What is stored Why and for how long
pf_consent Your decision about measurement, so the banner doesn’t ask you every time. Stored in your browser, valid for a year.
_ga and _ga_* Google Analytics cookies. Created only after consent, valid for two years.

From the analytics I see the pages visited, an approximate location at city level, the device type and where you came from. I don’t see your name or anything I could recognise you by. Google advertising features are switched off. You can change your decision at any time with the Cookie settings button in the footer.

05Your rights

The GDPR gives you several rights, and one email to me is enough to use them. I answer within a month and charge nothing for it.

  • Know what data I hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Have it corrected if it is wrong.
  • Have it deleted if I no longer have a reason to keep it.
  • Restrict the processing until something is cleared up.
  • Get it in a machine-readable form and take it elsewhere.
  • Object to the processing.

If you feel I’m handling your data badly, tell me directly first. If we don’t sort it out, you can complain to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, uoou.cz. You can also complain to the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live.

06Changes

If something changes on the site that affects your data, I rewrite this page and change the last updated date at the top.

Questions about your data or about a new website can go through the contact page. I answer within 24 hours.
